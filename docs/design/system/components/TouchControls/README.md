The on-screen pad for phones: three rows of round buttons that relabel themselves to say what they will do.

**Use** `.tb` (small) and `.tb.big` (PUNCH, KICK, JUMP). Color by job: default `neon` (attacks), `.gun` `gold`, `.spell` `mana`, `.move` `ok` (movement). Labels change in context: PUNCH becomes LIFT, THROW, KNEE, TALK or ENTER; GUN and SPELL show ammo and mana cost in a `small` line and dim (`.dim`) when unusable.

**Sizes** follow the player's setting: `control-face` and `control-small` at M (S and L in their notes). Hidden whenever a controller is in use.
